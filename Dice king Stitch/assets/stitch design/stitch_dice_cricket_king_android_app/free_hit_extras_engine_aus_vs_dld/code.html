Extras & Balance Guide

Section 10.0
Wide (W / W+1 / W+2) +1 Bowler Penalty

Delivery does not count toward the 6-ball over limit. +1 run added to batting team total and credited against bowler's economy. Striker may run additional byes if ball passes wicketkeeper.

No Ball (NB / NB+1) Free Hit Granted

Awarded for front-foot overstep, return crease cut, or above-waist full toss. Delivery does not count toward the over. Next ball is strictly a Free Hit where striker can only be dismissed run out.

Byes & Leg Byes (B1, L1, L2) Legal Ball Bowled

Delivery COUNTS as a legal ball bowled (over advances). Runs are counted in the team score total under 'Extras' and not charged to the bowler's personal runs conceded ledger.

8 Legal Dot Cards (Section 10.1) 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 11, 14, 16

In Faize Dice Games App Simulation mode, drawing any of these 8 cards converts the delivery into a completely legal 0-run Dot Ball. Protects 5-over games from becoming pure free-throw contests!
